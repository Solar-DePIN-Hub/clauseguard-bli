Read the fine print before it reads you.

ClauseGuard reads a contract the way a careful lawyer would — clause by clause — and shows you exactly which words are risky, the law behind each flag, and a fairer way to say it. No black box. Just the text, the rule, and the reason.

Residential Lease Agreement

1.

The Tenant shall pay the monthly rent of €1,200 on the first day of each month. Late payment after three (3) days shall incur a penalty of 5% of the monthly rent per day of delay.

a penalty of 5% of the monthly rent per day of delay Dir. 93/13/EEC Art. 3 — unfair term (excessive penalty)
2.

The Landlord may enter the premises at any time, without notice, for any purpose the Landlord deems appropriate.

enter the premises at any time, without notice Consumer Rights Dir. 2011/83/EU — right to quiet enjoyment
3.

In the event of any dispute, the Tenant waives all rights to bring a claim before a court and agrees that the Landlord's decision shall be final and binding.

the Tenant waives all rights to bring a claim before a court Dir. 93/13/EEC Art. 3(1)(e) — waiver of legal remedy
4.

The Tenant shall be solely liable for any and all repairs, including structural defects, at their own cost, regardless of cause.

solely liable for any and all repairs, including structural defects Dir. 93/13/EEC Art. 4 — imbalance of rights
5.

The Landlord may terminate this agreement at any time, for any reason, with no notice and no refund of prepaid rent.

terminate this agreement at any time, for any reason Consumer Rights Dir. 2011/83/EU — unfair termination

How it works

1) The text is split into sentences. 2) rules check for clauses a party needs (termination, metering, curtailment, force majeure, liability cap, data protection, payment terms…) and for risky wording (unilateral changes, one-sided liability exclusions, uncapped termination fees, tokens without securities/KYC disclosure). Rules only run when relevant (e.g. token rules only when tokens or investors are mentioned). 3) Score = share of applicable checks passed, weighted by severity. 4) Hashes are computed with Web Crypto. The same engine is available as POST /api/review for AI agents and the Chainlink CRE workflow in the repo.
